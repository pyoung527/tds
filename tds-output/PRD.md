## Product Summary

Launch List is a lightweight launch-readiness service for founders preparing to release a small product. It centralizes launch tasks, groups them by launch area, highlights blockers, and shows whether the launch is ready enough to proceed.

## Problem

Founders often spread launch work across notes, chat, spreadsheets, and memory, making it hard to know what remains, what is blocked, and what action should happen next.

## Target Users

- Founder: creates and manages the launch checklist, launch areas, collaborators, and readiness review.
- Collaborator: views launch tasks and updates assigned task status.

## Goals

- Founder can create a launch plan and first tasks within five minutes.
- Dashboard makes blocked tasks obvious.
- Launch work is grouped by product, marketing, legal, operations, and technical areas.
- Collaboration remains lightweight.

## Non-Goals

- Native mobile apps are not required for v1.
- Production code generation is out of scope.
- Complex readiness scoring is not required until the scoring decision is made.

## Success Metrics

- A founder can create a launch and add first tasks within five minutes.
- A founder can identify blocked tasks from the Dashboard.

## Risks

- Readiness score may feel untrustworthy if too abstract.
- Collaboration setup may be too heavy.

## Assumptions

- Assumption: account-based collaborators are acceptable unless invite-link access is approved.
- Assumption: initial readiness can be a transparent percentage plus blocked-task count.
- Assumption: task activity history can be deferred unless explicitly required.
