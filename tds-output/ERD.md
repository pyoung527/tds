## Entity Overview

```text
User 1 ──< Launch
Launch 1 ──< Launch Area

Launch Area 1 ──< Task
Task 0..1 ── assigned to ── User
```

## Entities

### User

Fields: id, name, email, role, created_at, updated_at.

Relationships: owns many Launches as Founder; can be assigned many Tasks.

### Launch

Fields: id, founder_user_id, name, target_date, status, created_at, updated_at.

Relationships: belongs to Founder/User; has many Tasks and Launch Areas.

### Launch Area

Fields: id, launch_id, name, description, sort_order, created_at, updated_at.

Relationships: belongs to Launch; has many Tasks.

### Task

Fields: id, launch_id, launch_area_id, assigned_user_id, title, description, status, due_date, blocker_note, created_at, updated_at.

Relationships: belongs to Launch and Launch Area; optionally assigned to User.

## Business Constraints

- Task status is pending, completed, or blocked.
- Blocked tasks require blocker_note.
- Blocked tasks are not counted as ready.
- Only Founders invite Collaborators.

## Persistence Assumptions

- Assumption: role may become a membership table if multi-launch collaborator access is needed.
- Assumption: task activity history and invite links are deferred until the related questions are answered.
